## What happened next

The site list was removed from the manifest description and the extension was resubmitted the same day ([e0724cd](https://github.com/LostBeard/AnaglyphoholV2/commit/e0724cd), [11b6484](https://github.com/LostBeard/AnaglyphoholV2/commit/11b6484e1383bbd9195a2029983a2794f80a755d)). The new description is the exact sentence Google already approved for version 3.0.14: "View internet videos and images in anaglyph 3D. Supports many popular sites with DRM-free media."
